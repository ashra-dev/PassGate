📖PassGate: Official System Manual
1. Introduction
PassGate is the official access and inventory portal for TIIKII-enabled events. This system provides a unified, secure way to track ticket custody, manage distributor inventory, and process benefit consumption at event stations.
2. Guide for: Distributor Admin (The Event Manager)
As the Event Manager, you are the architect of the event’s digital structure and the overseer of the supply chain.
System Initialization:
Navigate to the setup portal. Input your event parameters, including ticket tiers and benefit allowances.
Once initialized, the system creates the "Vault Pool"—the central repository from which all tickets originate.
Managing Distributors:
Use the Directory tab to register partner companies using their official email addresses. This registration grants them secure access to their own dedicated inventory portal.
Inventory Allocation:
Utilize the Serial Allocation Matrix to transfer ticket custody. By selecting specific ticket ranges (e.g., #100–#200), you assign those assets to a specific distributor, moving them out of the "Vault Pool."
System Monitoring:
Consult Admin Analytics to track performance and inventory movement.
Review the Benefit Scan Logs to audit usage and ensure all scans are occurring at the correct terminals.
3. Guide for: Distributors (The Asset Handlers)
Your role is to manage the inventory entrusted to your company and ensure your staff is prepared for the event.
Secure Access:
Login at the portal using your registered company email. You will receive a unique One-Time Password (OTP) via email; enter this to proceed.
Dashboard & Oversight:
Your dashboard provides a focused view of only the inventory assigned to your company. You cannot modify event settings or view the data of other distributors.
Inventory Management:
Regularly monitor your assigned tickets. Ensure that the inventory in your custody matches your physical records.
Benefit Tracking:
If your company manages specific benefit stations, ensure your on-site staff is trained to scan tickets accurately to track consumption in real-time.
4. Guide for: Station Users (The Gatekeepers)
You are the "boots on the ground" responsible for processing ticket holder entries and benefit redemptions.
The Scanning Interface:
When a guest presents their ticket, use the Authentication Terminal. You may use a scanner to read the barcode or the manual entry method if the ticket is damaged.
Interpreting Results:
Green: Access Granted. You may proceed with fulfilling the benefit.
Yellow: Benefit limit reached or already claimed. Redirect the guest to an Admin for verification.
Red: Access Denied. The ticket may be invalid or not authorized for this specific station.
Operational Integrity:
Remember that the system is synchronized globally. Once a benefit is scanned as "used" at your station, it is updated instantly across the entire system.
Maintenance: Keep your terminal powered and connected. In the event of a technical failure, report it immediately to your Distributor representative.
